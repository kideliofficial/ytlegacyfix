YT Legacy Fix

Work on iOS 7 ONLY. I have not tried it on iOS 8 or 9. This tweak fixes the YouTube app for iOS 7 instead of having to download an older version or some random ass janky YouTube client. It is very much a WIP since my main focus was to get video playback working on the app first. Any help would be appreciated.
